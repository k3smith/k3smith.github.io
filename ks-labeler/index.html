Instructions (about 3 minutes) — please read once

Mark contiguous spans that are Knowledge or Skill building blocks. Tools and techniques count (CAD, GD&T, calipers) — not only full learning-objective sentences. Subject expertise helps but is optional; judge from the sentence alone.

Knowledge Something someone possesses — facts, principles, standards, tools, platforms, languages, or domain fields (ESCO / SkillSpan-style). Examples: GD&T, ISO 9000, Python, iOS and Android development, AWS. Tech stacks and “X development” name knowledge of those platforms, not a skip.
Skill Applying know-how to do work — usually verb-led (hard or soft). Examples: operate a lathe, use calipers, apply size tolerances, strong communication skills, ability to program CNC (trainable skill, not an innate ability). Prefer Skill over Knowledge only when the span is clearly “doing,” not naming a tool/platform.
Do not mark Course chores (read chapter 3, watch the video, submit the quiz), file/module names, job titles alone, or traits training cannot develop (innate dexterity, color vision). Mark the shortest useful span; drop fluff like “advanced knowledge of” / “proficient in.” If nothing qualifies, use No K/S here.

Worked examples

Colored underlines show what you would paint. Mark the shortest useful span that names the knowledge or skill — not the whole sentence unless the whole sentence is the span.

Manufacturing training · mark tools & practices

Students will use calipers and apply GD&T when inspecting parts.

Skill: use calipers · Knowledge: GD&T · Leave “Students will … when inspecting parts” unmarked.

Manufacturing training · machine operation

Operate lathes and milling machines to produce simple cylindrical parts.

One skill span is fine when the verbs share one practice. “Produce simple cylindrical parts” is the task outcome — optional to mark only if it clearly states a trainable skill by itself.

Job / training · platforms & soft skill

Looking for someone with Python , iOS and Android development , and strong communication skills.

Languages, platforms, and “X development” → Knowledge. Soft / applied phrases → Skill. Do not mark “Looking for someone with”.

Do not mark · activity / homework

Read Chapter 4 and submit the Excel homework by Friday.

No Knowledge/Skill spans. Choose No K/S here.

Borderline · prefer the trainable reading

Must have the ability to read blueprints.

Mark as skill (apply know-how). Do not skip it as an “ability” — we only exclude non-trainable traits.
