Matrix Multiplication Visualizer: Row Times Column

ENGT 305 · Module 2  |  Each entry of a matrix product is one row of the left matrix dotted with one column of the right matrix. Step through and watch the product fill in.

Key idea: To get the entry in row i, column j of the product, take row i of the left matrix and column j of the right matrix, multiply them pair by pair, and add. That sum of pairwise products is a dot product. It only works if the row and the column have the same number of entries, which is the dimension rule.

In practice: A set of linear equations is written as A x = b, where each row of A times the column x is the left side of one equation. That is exactly the row-times-column product shown here, so when MATLAB solves the system with x = A\b, you can check the answer by confirming that A*x reproduces b. In MATLAB, A*B is this matrix product and A.*B is the element-wise product (each entry times the entry in the same position, so the two sizes must be equal). They are different operations and usually give different answers. Order also matters: switch to B × A and compare.
