Last two digits = carbon content in hundredths of a percent (xx40 → 0.40% C). Five-digit codes mean ≥1.00% C: 52100 = chromium steel, 1.00% C (bearing steel).
Why it matters: in this course you'll read designations like "AISI 4140 OQT 1100" dozens of times. The code tells you chemistry (family + carbon); the condition tells you heat treatment. Decode both and you can predict properties before you ever open the appendix.
