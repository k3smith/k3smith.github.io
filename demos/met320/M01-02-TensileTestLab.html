What to notice: the machine only ever measures two things — force and extension. Everything on the right is those two numbers divided by constants (A₀ and L₀). Compare materials: steel announces yielding with a plateau; aluminum never does (that's why the 0.2% offset exists); cast iron gives no warning at all; rubber isn't even trying to be linear.
