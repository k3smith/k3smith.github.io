Instructions (about 1 minute)

A learning objective is a short statement of what a learner should be able to do or know after studying a section.

You will see two statements from the same textbook section:

  • Candidate — produced by an automated system
  • Course — written by the course/textbook author

Decide whether they describe the same learning goal. You are not grading writing quality, grammar, or whether the wording uses “action verbs.” Subject expertise is optional — judge from the two statements alone.

Same A learner who achieved one has essentially achieved the other. Paraphrase is fine if the target skill or knowledge is the same.
Partially overlapping Related topic, or one is broader/narrower, or they only overlap in part. Achieving one would not clearly mean achieving the other.
Different Distinct goals, or the candidate is not really stating a learning objective for this course statement.

If stuck between Same and Partial, choose Partial. Work in short batches (10–20). You can close the tab and return later — use the same name/initials so we do not show you pairs you already judged. Each pair is shown to up to two different people; the site picks an available pair at random for you. Shortcuts: 1 / 2 / 3.
