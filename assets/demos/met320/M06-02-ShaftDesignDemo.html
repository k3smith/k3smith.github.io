The gear's transmitted force bends the shaft while the transmitted torque twists it between the gear and the coupling at the right end. Find the smallest safe diameter at the gear — that's where bending moment peaks.

The design chain: Gear force → bearing reactions → M diagram → dmin at the critical section → standard size → bearing bore must match! Slide the gear toward a bearing: the moment falls (shorter lever arm) but that bearing's reaction climbs — smaller shaft, bigger bearing. Shaft design and bearing selection are one coupled problem, which is why Milestone 3 treats them together.
