Explore the coupling: Halve the wire diameter and the rate drops by 16× (k ∝ d⁴) while stress soars (τ ∝ 1/d³). Add coils to soften the spring — but the solid length grows and you lose working stroke. Stretch Lf past 5.2·Dm and the buckling flag trips, just like a slender column. Every spring design is a negotiation among rate, stress, stroke, and stability.
