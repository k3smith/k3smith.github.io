Reading the diagram: The Goodman line connects Sn′ (fully reversed) to Su (static). Points below the line survive indefinitely; the ratio of the line intercept to the operating point along the load line is the safety factor N. Soderberg replaces Su with Sy — safer, but often overly conservative. The yield line guards against first-cycle yielding at high mean stress.
