⚙️ Spur Gear Mesh — Involute Action, Contact Ratio & Interference

MET 320 · Module 4  |  Change tooth counts, module, and pressure angle; watch the line of action and contact ratio respond

Pitch circles (tangent at pitch point P) Base circles (involute origin) Line of action (at pressure angle φ)
Key idea: Involute teeth transmit force along a straight line of action tangent to both base circles. The contact ratio tells you the average number of tooth pairs in contact — below 1.2, expect noise and shock. Too few pinion teeth causes interference (the gear tip digs into the pinion flank below the base circle).

Experiment: Drop the pinion to 12 teeth at φ = 20° and watch the interference warning — then fix it by switching to φ = 25° (larger base circle clearance) or adding pinion teeth. Notice the contact ratio falls as pressure angle rises: the trade-off is stronger teeth vs. smoother, quieter action. Module m only scales size — it never changes VR, contact ratio, or interference.
