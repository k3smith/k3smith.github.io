Watch for the trade-off: Shrinking the small sheave raises the ratio without a big sheave — but it also cuts the wrap angle and belt speed, and belt life drops sharply below ~75 mm. Increasing C improves wrap but adds belt length and vibration. In Module 3 you'll formalize this: L = 2C + 1.57(D₂+D₁) + (D₂−D₁)²/4C, then pick a standard belt and recompute C.
